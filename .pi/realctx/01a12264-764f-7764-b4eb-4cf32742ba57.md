`scripts/hwtest.sh [timeout_s]` creates `.hwtest/venv` (greenlet cffi pyserial jinja2), copies `config/printer-joker.cfg`, starts klippy from `$KLIPPER` (default /mnt/ssd/data/git/klipper) via `setsid /usr/bin/sg dialout`, and waits for "Configured MCU 'mcu'". Klippy keeps running; stop with `kill -- -$(cat .hwtest/klippy.pid)` (process group, since killing the sg pid alone leaves python holding the serial lock). Rerunning the script kills the old one. Log: `.hwtest/klippy.log`, gcode pty: `.hwtest/printer`. Replaces old /tmp/k5 and /tmp/kvenv.
